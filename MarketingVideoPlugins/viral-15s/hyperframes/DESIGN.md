# Tappy Viral 15 Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A high-energy Apple-style product reveal staged inside a cozy desk setup: crisp silver surfaces, deep charcoal UI, and one soft pink activation glow. The first three seconds feel deliberately lifeless and still; the Tappy activation at three seconds introduces depth, snap, and tactile motion. UI remains clean and believable while typography carries the social-video energy.

## Colors

- `#FFFFFF` - crisp Apple silver and CTA surfaces
- `#1A1A1A` - deep charcoal canvas and UI
- `#FFB6C1` - Tappy activation glow and primary accent
- `#E7E7EC` - secondary silver surfaces
- `#5A3FD6` - app-icon violet, used only where the real Tappy identity appears

## Typography

- Inter Black (`900`) for hooks and kinetic headlines
- Inter Medium/Semibold (`500-700`) for UI labels and supporting copy
- Letter spacing remains `0`

## Motion

- Use strong Apple-like deceleration for UI entrances: `power4.out` and `expo.out`
- Use `back.out(1.35)` only for the activation toggle and carousel snaps
- Key and audio accents must land on explicit sound timestamps
- Scene changes use a soft pink light band or silver wipe; never use a bare jump cut
- Keep the hook scene nearly motionless so the activation contrast is unmistakable

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use purple-blue full-screen gradients, floating orbs, or bokeh decoration
- Do not use fake testimonials, feature claims, or obsolete windowed-app UI
- Do not place text beneath social-platform safe margins
- Do not overlap captions with the product UI
- Do not use generic stock photography when the Tappy desk image is available
