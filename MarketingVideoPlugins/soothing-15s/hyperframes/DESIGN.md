# Tappy Soothing ASMR Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A minimal, quiet macOS product film with the calm precision of an Apple interface and the tactile warmth of an ASMR desk setup. Large areas of negative space, restrained blush accents, gentle ivory surfaces, and slow rounded motion keep attention on the sound itself. Wet Squishy Pop feels soft and elastic; Wax Cracking feels crisp and delicate.

## Colors

- `#151416` - warm charcoal canvas
- `#F7F6F3` - soft ivory surface
- `#FFB6C1` - blush ASMR accent
- `#C9B9FF` - Tappy icon lavender accent
- `#666168` - quiet secondary text

## Typography

- Inter Black (`900`) for short headlines
- Inter Medium/Semibold (`500-700`) for supporting copy and UI
- Letter spacing remains `0`

## Motion

- Favor `sine.inOut`, `power2.out`, and gentle `power3.out` easing
- Entrances take `0.6-0.9s` and use small travel distances
- ASMR visual pulses land exactly on the recorded sound hits
- Scene transitions are soft solid-color veils, never flashes
- Keep the final quarter-second fully black

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use fast typing montages, aggressive zooms, or hard strobe flashes
- Do not use dense card grids, decorative orbs, bokeh, or gradient text
- Do not add sounds that are not part of Tappy's current bundled packs
- Do not crowd the App Store call to action
- Do not show obsolete windowed-app UI
