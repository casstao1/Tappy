# Tappy Video Identity

## Style Prompt

A tactile, premium macOS product film built around the physical pleasure of sound. The canvas is deep violet-black with localized lavender light from the Tappy key icon, crisp pale typography, restrained translucent surfaces, and brief warm accents for different sound textures. Motion should feel keyed and responsive: quick compression, soft spring release, deliberate wipes, and audio-like pulses rather than floating decoration.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#0B0913`
- Surface: `#17141F`
- Primary text: `#F4F1FA`
- Tappy violet: `#8A6BFF`
- Soft lavender: `#C9BBFF`
- Active green: `#4BDD79`
- Tactile coral: `#FF7B78`

## Typography

- Display: `SF Pro Display`, 700-800 weight
- Interface/body: `SF Pro Text`, 400-600 weight

## Motion

- Fast key-down compression followed by a soft spring release.
- Scene changes use full-frame violet cover wipes.
- Interface elements enter with short, varied transforms and settle cleanly.
- Decorative rings and meters pulse finitely and slowly behind the primary motion.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use purple-to-blue full-screen gradients.
- Do not use floating orb decoration or bokeh.
- Do not use tiny desktop-sized text that becomes unreadable in a vertical social video.
- Do not fabricate testimonials or usage numbers.
- Do not show the obsolete windowed Tappy interface.
